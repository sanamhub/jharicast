OpenStreetMap
  Used in: src/Jharicast.Nepal/Towns.cs (town names and positions, each row cites its node id),
    shipped in the Jharicast.Nepal and Jharicast.Cli packages
  Licence: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  Note: the town list is a derived database under the ODbL. Changes to it stay under the ODbL.

Administrative boundaries
  Used in: src/Jharicast.Routing/Data/districts.json (district polygons), shipped in the
    Jharicast.Routing and Jharicast.Cli packages
  Source: Survey Department of Nepal and UN RCO Nepal, via OCHA / HDX (cod-ab-npl, v02,
    npl_admin2.geojson, files dated 2026-08-14), https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-npl
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution for Intergovernmental Organisations (CC BY-IGO) 3.0,
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/legalcode (licence read from the HDX
    dataset metadata on 2026-09-26)
  Modified: each outer ring simplified with Douglas-Peucker at 0.002 degrees (about 200 m),
    coordinates rounded to 5 decimals, names replaced by Jharicast district ids, all other
    attributes dropped, and written as JSON (tools/districts/build-districts.cs).

Test fixtures (not in any package)
  Used in: tests/Jharicast.Tests/Fixtures and tests/Jharicast.Cli.Tests/Fixtures
  The files named dhm-*, bipad-* and open-meteo-* are responses captured from the public pages
  and APIs of the Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM), Nepal, the BIPAD portal
  (NDRRMA) and Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). They remain their owners' data and are kept only so the
  parsers can be tested. osrm-route-docs-example.json is the Route example of OSRM's HTTP API
  documentation, re-encoded as polyline6. The files named *-synthetic.json and those under Fixtures/replay that
  are not links to the captures are made up, with fake values.
