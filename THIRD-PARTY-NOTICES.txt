Administrative boundaries
  Used in: src/Jharicast.Routing/Data/districts.json (district polygons)
  Source: Survey Department of Nepal and UN RCO Nepal, via OCHA / HDX (cod-ab-npl, v02,
    npl_admin2.geojson, files dated 2026-08-14), https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-npl

    coordinates rounded to 5 decimals, names replaced by Jharicast district ids, all other
    attributes dropped, and written as JSON (tools/districts/build-districts.cs).
